# Community Code of Conduct

Participate respectfully. Give specific, constructive feedback. Welcome people regardless of background or experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or impersonate others.

Keep discussions relevant to the project. Respect licenses and attribution. Never post live credentials or private user data. Maintainers may moderate content and participation to protect the community, with proportionate actions and explanations where practical.
